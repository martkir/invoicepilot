# Share flow v3: the audit

The diagnosis that produced this folder. It reads
[`../share-flow-v2`](../share-flow-v2/index.html) against
[`.claude/skills/mercury-ui-light`](../../../.claude/skills/mercury-ui-light/SKILL.md)
and says what had to change, what had to stay, and what the change costs.

This is the light twin of [`../share-flow-v3`](../share-flow-v3/index.html).
Every finding below applies to both folders; the passages that name a theme
direction are the only ones that differ, and they are marked where they
matter.

The flow itself is not under review here. What the share flow does, and what it
deliberately does not do, is still specified in
[`../share-flow/design.md`](../share-flow/design.md); v2's four added states are
still specified in [`../share-flow-v2/design.md`](../share-flow-v2/design.md).
Neither moved.

---

## The read

**Surface.** A share and recipient-facing page, plus two owner screens on the
existing dashboard, plus a transactional email, plus the two review shells
(contact sheet, viewer) that are documentation rather than product.

**Mode.** Re-skin, in the Section 8.A sense: existing content and information
architecture, new visual language. Routes, filenames, copy voice, data model
and the fourteen screens are preserved exactly. (This is the audit of the
re-skin, and it describes what v3 changed against v2. Revision 2 later added a
screen and renamed another; that is logged in
[`../share-flow-v3-light-annotated/revisions.md`](../share-flow-v3-light-annotated/revisions.md),
not here.) What changes is everything the
tokens touch, plus four structural decisions that only made sense in the system
v2 was built on.

**Dial reading of v2.** `DESIGN_VARIANCE` 5 (one asymmetric split inside an
otherwise stacked column, plus the overlap), `MOTION_INTENSITY` 4 (two
perpetual loops, a spring easing, three entry animations),
`VISUAL_DENSITY` 5.

**Dials for v3.** `DESIGN_VARIANCE` 6, `MOTION_INTENSITY` 3,
`VISUAL_DENSITY` 6. That is Section 2.A's redesign-overhaul row applied to the
reading above, with motion moving *down* rather than up because two of v2's
four points came from animation the system bans outright.

**Delivery target.** DRAFT. Static HTML generated by `build.py`, opening from
`file://` with no server and no build step. Section 3's `[PRODUCTION]` rules
(framework, bundler, npm icon libraries, self-hosted fonts, RSC) are out of
scope; every rule in Sections 1, 2, 4, 6, 7 and 8 applies in full.

## What v2 got right, and v3 keeps

Most of the diagnosis in
[`../share-flow-v2/audit.md`](../share-flow-v2/audit.md) is about structure
rather than surface, and structure survives a change of design system intact.

- **One page for the owner and the recipient.** No what-they-will-see mode to
  keep in step with what they actually see.
- **The filename is the largest thing on the page.** "Is this the right
  export?" is the only question the page has to settle before anyone clicks.
- **The page is laid out as the zip's contents.** A `pdf` part and a `csv`
  part under a `zip` chip, so no sentence has to say what is in the download.
- **The manifest is a sheet, not a working surface.** Nothing in it is
  operable, because nothing in it is decidable. Sticky header, tabular mono,
  the zip's own entry names, counts on the last line.
- **The ninth column counts what the CSV has and the page does not**, and it
  stays true across breakpoints because each dropped column moves into the
  count.
- **The composer asks for one thing** and shows the real draft in an iframe
  rather than a picture of it.
- **Four states v1 never drew**: loading, downloading, invalid address, 404.
- **Semantics and a11y**: landmarks, one `<h1>`, a skip link, `aria-busy`,
  `aria-invalid` with `aria-describedby`, `aria-disabled` instead of a dead
  button, a real `<title>`, description, og block and favicon on every screen.

None of that was touched. Section 8.C says a re-skin is not a content rewrite,
and the only copy that changed is the copy that had to (see *Dashes*, below).

## What conflicts with Mercury, and what replaced it

### 1. The palette changes hue, not brightness

v2: warm off-white canvas, oxide red `#A8382C`, a warm grey family, pink-free
but firmly light.

Mercury light is `scheme: light`, and Section 4.12 makes that a brand decision
rather than a default: no dark mode, no toggle, no `prefers-color-scheme`
branch. This twin therefore keeps v2's brightness and changes everything else
about the palette: the warm greys go cool (`--page` becomes `#eef0f6`,
`--white` becomes `#fbfcfd`), and the oxide red becomes `#5266eb`.

The one thing that does not survive is v2's **three-level text grey**. Mercury
ships exactly two text colours, `--ink` and `--ink-soft`, and
`--hairline-strong` fails AA as body text on the canvas (2.7:1 here). So
`--ink-2` and `--muted` both alias `--ink-soft`, and the third level of
hierarchy comes from size instead of from another grey. Every value on screen
is now one of two colours, which is a stricter system than v2 had and a legible
one: 14.5:1 and 6.0:1 on the canvas.

### 2. Flatness is law

Deleted, not re-tinted:

| v2 | Why it is gone |
| --- | --- |
| `--sh-1`, `--sh-2`, `--sh-3` on every card, button, popover and leaf | Section 4.4: no `box-shadow` on any element, ever |
| `--edge`, the 1px inner highlight | Same rule; it is an inset shadow |
| The fixed 3.5% `feTurbulence` grain over the page and inside the band | Section 4.4: no grain or noise overlay, no texture ornament |
| Radial accent washes on `body`, `.batch` and the audit panel | Same rule: no ambient gradients |
| `linear-gradient` on the primary button, the brand mark, the progress bar and `.part-docs` | Same |
| `backdrop-filter: blur(14px)` on the owner bar and the viewer's help scrim | Section 4.4: no glassmorphism, no frosted panel |
| The focus ring's `box-shadow` halo | Section 4.4; the outline alone does the job |

All four shadow tokens are kept as names resolving to `none`, so any rule
inherited from v2 that still asks for one gets nothing rather than a shadow.

**What replaces them is contrast and space.** `--canvas` behind `--paper`
behind `--paper-hi` is three legible steps, and the sheet's rows are separated
by a hairline derived from `--hairline-strong` rather than by a surface trick.

### 3. Two structural decisions were load-bearing on shadows

These are the changes that make v3 a redesign rather than a repaint.

**The overlap.** v2's one layout rule was that exactly one element lifts: the
document card overlapped the header band by 44px. That reads as a foreground
only because three layered shadows say which card is in front. Flat, a
negative margin is just a misaligned element.

*Replaced by* a two-column page. A 320px rail carries the identity (chip,
filename, four facts), the one action, and for the owner the live state, the
name the link goes out under and the send; the right column carries the
composer and the manifest. The rail is sticky, so the answer and the action
stay on screen through 37 rows. That also retires v2's **sticky owner bar** and
the `--sticky-top` variable the sheet's own sticky header had to read off the
page, because what it held now lives in something that is already sticky.

**The fan.** v2 drew the five thumbnails as overlapped sheets of paper, each
rotated about a degree off square. Take the shadows away and it is six rotated
rectangles in a pile.

*Replaced by* six tiles for six things: five documents and the count of the
other 31 (v2's "+32" was counting invoices under a heading that said
documents). Each tile renders what the product actually shows when the page-1
thumbnail is missing, which is the vendor's initials on a plain panel;
production drops an `<img>` into the same box. That is a real product state
rather than a mock-up of a document, which is what Section 4.9 rules out.

### 4. Motion drops from 4 to 3

- The **"live" dot pulsed** on a 2.6s infinite loop. Section 5 bans perpetual
  pulse loops; the dot stays, static, because it carries real state and
  Section 7.A allows one per section on exactly that condition.
- The **skeleton shimmered** on a 1.4s infinite loop for the length of the
  request. Same rule. The shape of the placeholder already reads as *not here
  yet*, and `aria-busy` says it to everyone else.
- The **spring easing** (`cubic-bezier(.34, 1.36, .5, 1)`) overshoots, and
  Mercury ships two easings, neither of which does. Entry animations use
  `--ease-emphasized` at `--d-base`; everything else is `--ease-standard` at
  `--d-fast`.
- What remains is one shared 300ms rise for the popover, the composer, the
  From menu and the sent banner, plus state transitions on every control. Both
  are motivated in one sentence each, which is the Section 5 test.

### 5. Type

- **Weights above 500 are gone.** v2 ran 550, 600, 650 and 700 (the fan's
  vendor marks were 700). Section 4.1: hierarchy comes from size, tracking and
  a 400 to 500 step, never from heavy weights.
- **Upper case is gone**, which also removes the file-type chips' last
  justification for it. `zip`, `pdf` and `csv` are lower case, in mono, which
  is how they appear in the filename beside them anyway.
- **Positive tracking is gone.** v2's `--track-label: 0.02em` sat on every
  small label; the token is deleted rather than zeroed, so it cannot come back
  under a name that invites it.
- **Nothing is below 12px.** v2 went to 9.5px on the chips and the fan's vendor
  line, and 10.5px in several places. Mercury's smallest step is
  `caption-md` at 12px, and it is now the floor.
- **A third family is gone.** v2's contact sheet set its headings in Fraunces.
  Section 4.1 allows Geist and Geist Mono and nothing else, including in the
  documentation around the screens.

### 6. Radii

Mercury's scale, spent by one rule (Section 4.4): interactive is `--r-pill`,
containers are `--r-lg`, things inside other things are `--r-sm` or `--r-xs`.
That converts icon buttons, the pager, the update button, the add-source
button, inputs and the link row into pills, and drops badges to `--r-xs`
because a pill-shaped non-interactive badge misreads as a button.

One documented exception: **the checkbox keeps `--r-xs`**. A pill checkbox is a
circle, a circle is a radio button, and a multi-select control that looks
single-select is a worse failure than an inconsistent radius.

### 7. Colour discipline on the dashboard

- **Ten vendor brand colours, inline on every row and every fan leaf, are
  gone.** Ten saturated fills is ten accents; Section 4.2's consistency lock
  allows one. The vendor is identified by its name in the cell
  beside the mark.
- **Gmail's four-colour glyph** is now single-colour, per Section 4.9's rule
  for logos.
- **The accent is rationed** to the real actions - Share in the toolbar,
  Download all and Send on the share page - their equivalents in the popover,
  the progress bar and the focus ring. Share is the dashboard's only filled
  shape, which is what marks it as the screen's one primary action; every other
  toolbar control is an outline or a bare glyph. The sorted-column marker, the
  current page in the pager and the open row, all three of which were drawn in
  the accent, are now carried by `--ink` against `--ink-soft` or by a surface
  step, because they are states rather than actions. The tint that is left is a
  ticked row, which is a selection and keeps it.
- **The email's branded band was a slab of the accent.** It is now
  `--canvas-deep`, and the mail's one accent is its one button.

### 8. Errors needed a colour of their own

v2 drew both error states in the accent. That read correctly only because the
accent was a red; against an electric blue it says nothing. Section 1.B is the
answer: exactly one status colour, defined once in the tokens file, used only
in a genuine error.

`--danger: #b3261e` holds 5.7:1 on `--canvas` and 6.4:1 on `--paper`. It
appears in exactly two places in the whole flow: the invalid address and the
failed send. (The dark twin uses `#ff6b6b` for the same job. A light red is
2.4:1 on this canvas and a dark red is 3.2:1 on that one, so this is the one
token whose *value* cannot be shared between the twins, only its role.) There is no success colour and no warning colour, which is why
v2's decision to kill the green never had to be revisited.

### 9. Dashes

Section 7.D bans the em dash and the en dash outright, in every visible
string. v2 spent them freely, including the three below. (The two code spans
in this list quote v2 verbatim and are the only place either character appears
anywhere in this folder; a grep that skips this section returns nothing.)

- as the **"no value" glyph in the manifest**, which is data, so the hyphen is
  now doing that job;
- in the **subject line** (`Invoices — Apr–Jun 2026`), which is copy the
  product sends on someone's behalf, now `Invoices, Apr to Jun 2026`;
- in the **period** (`Apr 1 – Jun 30`), where a plain hyphen is the correct
  range separator anyway.

Everything else was restructured into two sentences, a comma, a colon or
parentheses. The generated screens, the contact sheet, the viewer and the mail
contain zero of either character.

### 10. Smaller things the audit turned up

- **The form.** Labels ran down the left of their fields on a fixed 52px
  column, which is a settings row, not a form. Section 4.6 wants the label
  above the input; it is.
- **A status dot with no state.** `2 accounts connected` carried a coloured
  dot that never changed. Section 7.A: zero by default.
- **Off-by-one.** The fan's tail said `+32` under a heading that said
  36 documents. Five documents shown leaves 31.
- **Two flex containers with inline `<b>` children** opened stray gaps around
  the emphasised words. Both are blocks now.
- **Dead-end footers** carried the batch's two footnotes on pages that have no
  batch. Expired and not-found now get the legal links and nothing else.

## What this costs

Worth stating plainly, because a redesign that only lists wins is a sales
document.

- **The vendor marks are less scannable.** Ten brand colours were a real
  wayfinding aid in a 37-row table; neutral initials are not. The system
  allows one accent and this is what that costs. If it turns out to matter,
  the honest fix is a vendor column with a logo, not ten fills.
- **The rail is a bigger structural change than a re-skin usually earns.** It
  is justified by the overlap being unbuildable flat, but it is the one place
  where v3 changes the page rather than its surface, and it should be reviewed
  as a layout decision rather than waved through as a token change.
- **Light is a commitment.** Mercury light has no dark mode by design, so a
  recipient reading at night gets a bright page and no way to dim it. That is
  the deal, and it is the mirror of the one the dark twin makes.
- **`--primary-bright` has nowhere to sit.** A tint lighter than the accent is
  2.7 to 3.3:1 on these surfaces, so the token is carried for parity with the
  dark twin and never spent. Non-text marks take `--primary` instead.
- **Two derived tokens do not come from Section 1.** `--rule` and `--paper-hi`
  are `color-mix` compositions of `--hairline-strong`, `--ink` and `--paper`,
  written that way so they cannot drift, but they exist because Mercury's
  single `--hairline` value is the same hex as `--paper` and a divider inside
  a card needs to be visible. It is documented in `tokens-v3.css` rather than
  improvised per screen.

## Pre-flight check

Section 10, run against the generated output. Boxes tagged `[PRODUCTION]` are
skipped and named as skipped, per the instruction at the foot of that section.

**System fidelity**

- [x] Design read declared, dials explicit: share flow, re-skin overhaul, 6 / 3 / 6, DRAFT.
- [x] Every colour, spacing, radius and font value comes from a token. The only literals are the email's inlined values, which Section 1.A exempts, and the two `color-mix` derivations in `tokens-v3.css`.
- [x] `--primary` on at most two actions per screen; never text, never decoration.
- [x] Zero `box-shadow`, zero `backdrop-filter`, zero gradients, zero grain. Verified by grep across the folder.
- [x] Radius rule followed, with the checkbox exception documented above.
- [x] Light throughout. No dark section, no toggle, no `dark:` pair. The viewer's modal scrim is the canvas at 88%, not a dark veil.
- [x] Geist and Geist Mono only; no weight above 500.
- [x] Every figure in `--num` with `tabular-nums`.
- [x] No all-caps, no positive tracking.

**Interaction and a11y**

- [x] `cursor: pointer` on every clickable element, `cursor: text` on the one field.
- [x] Focus ring on every interactive element, `2px solid var(--primary)`.
- [x] Contrast: ink 14.5:1, ink-soft 6.0:1, on-primary over primary 4.7:1 and over primary-deep 6.9:1, primary-deep over accent-pale-blue 5.0:1, danger 5.7:1 on canvas and 6.4:1 on paper. All past AA. The primary button **darkens** on hover: `--on-primary` over `--primary-bright` is 3.4:1 and fails, which is a defect this folder found in the skill's own component recipe and which Section 4.2 now records.
- [x] No CTA wraps; longest primary label is two words.
- [x] No two CTAs share an intent; "Send by email" is one label used in both places it appears.
- [x] Empty, loading and error states all drawn as their own files.
- [x] No component cliches: no three-tower anything, no carousel, no modal, no pill-shaped badge.
- [x] Strategic omissions covered: privacy and terms in the footer, a way back from every screen, a custom 404, client-side validation, a favicon on every page.
- [x] Touch targets at 44px minimum: `.btn`, `.btn-quiet` and `.field input` all carry `min-height: 44px`.
- [x] Reduced motion honoured (`reskin.css`, and again in the two one-off pages).
- [x] Semantic landmarks, one `<h1>` per screen, skip link, `sr-only` captions on the tile strip and the sheet.

**Layout**

- [x] Section 4.8's hero rules do not apply: these are app and system screens with a header, not a marketing hero (Section 9).
- [x] Zero eyebrows.
- [x] No split-header pattern.
- [x] No consecutive image-and-text splits; the one split on the page appears once.
- [x] Grid cell count matches item count: six tiles, six things.
- [x] Nav is one line at desktop, 64px.
- [x] Every multi-column layout declares its own collapse (1100, 900, 780, 640).
- [x] `min-h-[100dvh]`, grid rather than flex percentage math.

**Content**

- [x] Zero em dashes and zero en dashes. Verified by grep.
- [x] Copy self-audit run over every visible string.
- [x] No AI cliche verbs, no "Oops", no exclamation marks, sentence case headers.
- [x] No placeholder identities: the vendors are real companies, the amounts are net plus VAT figures that add up, the dates vary.
- [x] No quotes or testimonials on any screen.
- [x] No div-based fake screenshots. The tiles render the product's own missing-thumbnail state; the email preview is the real email in an iframe.
- [x] No logo wall.

**Motion**

- [x] Every animation justifiable in one sentence: the popover, the composer, the From menu and the banner all say "this just arrived".
- [x] Nothing pins, hijacks, marquees or loops.
- [x] No scroll listeners; only `transform` and `opacity` animate.
- [x] One icon set, one 24px viewBox, one 1.6 stroke, emitted from one helper.

**Draft**
